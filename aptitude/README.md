# Aptitude

A local course for the arithmetic that shows up on campus, bank, and SSC papers. Ten chapters, each with notes, a shortcut, a picture you can move, and at least 100 questions.

Progress stays in this browser under `aptitude-v1`. Skills you miss are ranked by how often they fail, and weak-spot practice prefers those skills.

Open `index.html`. Check the ranking with `node test.js`.
